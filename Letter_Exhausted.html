You're Exhausted / Overwhelmed 🌷

Hellloooo Akkaaaa ❤️

If you're reading this, I'm guessing you're having one of those days where everything feels overwhelming, and you need someone to remind you that you can get through it.

Sooooo, here I am. ❤️

You are a "Wonder Woman" for real. I have always admired the way you handle everything. Even when things are not actually easy, you somehow make them look easy, and that takes another level of energy.

You make it look so effortless that people don't always realise how much effort you put in just to stay calm and handle everything so maturely. Everyone trusts you to manage any situation beautifully, and you absolutely do.

But even the best of the best people need a break sometimes. They need to stop, breathe, and just be.

So whenever you feel exhausted, or you're simply too tired to even pray, please don't feel bad about it. Being good at handling things doesn't mean that things are easy for you.

"The parts we can't tell, we carry them well. But that doesn't mean they're not heavy."

Maybe people don't always understand what you're carrying, but I do. And whenever the world feels too heavy to carry, you can always text me.

I will always be ready to listen to all your worries, random thoughts, complaints, and overwhelming thoughts with my professional "Hmmss" like Marudhu from Modha Raatri. 😂❤️

So just remember to breathe.

You don't have to prove anything to anyone.

We have the most amazing Writer writing our story, and He will do His miracles in HIS TIME. ❤️

And if you're too tired to pray, it's okay. I'll pray from my side for you.

You are an AMAZING PERSON. You have come soooo far.

You moved away from home, you're working double shifts, studying, managing so many responsibilities, and yet you never miss an opportunity to make us smile with your mischief.

That is such an amazing talent of yours... but I know sometimes it can also become a burden.

I'm pretty sure there are times when you put on a smile during our video calls while carrying a lot more than you let us see.

I just want you to know that you don't always have to hide it from me.

I'm always here. ❤️

And whenever you feel too overwhelmed, remember the little reminder from OK Not To Be OK — you don't have to go through those moments alone. ❤️

So here's your reminder:

If you're tired, stop.
Stop everything.
Take a break.

Sleep. Cry. Sit quietly. Do absolutely nothing if that's what you need.

And then, when you're ready, start again.

You don't have to take the shortest break or the quickest break. It doesn't matter how long it takes.

And if you don't know what to do...

TEXT ME.

(Because I always have my phone on mute 😭😂)

But seriously, Akka, you don't have to carry everything alone.

You can always come home to us — even if it's just through a phone call, a random text, or one of our stupid conversations.

And if you are too exhausted to remind yourself that everything will be okay...

I'll remind you. ❤️

With all my love,
Sheryl ♡
