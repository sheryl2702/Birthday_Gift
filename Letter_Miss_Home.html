You Miss Home ❤️

Hiii Akkaaa ❤️

If you are reading this letter, then you must be missing home.

And let me tell you something... the home misses you too.

It isn't the same without you here. Every moment we spend at home feels like it has a little missing piece — and that piece is you.

But remember one thing:

Far or near,
you are always in our minds. ❤️

I miss you a lot too.

You made home so much more lively with all your silly mischiefs.

I miss you when I play Super Mario Bros, because seriously... who is Luigi without his Mario? 😭😂

Whenever I go shopping and I don't have anyone to suggest clothes to me, I miss you.

I miss you when I'm watching a movie that I know you would love.

And I miss you extra when one of our favourite songs plays.

🎵 Our Songs “Unmai kaadhal yaarendral Unnai ennai solvenae Neeyum naanum poi endraal Kadhalai thedi kolvenae”
— the song that will always remind me of us ❤️

But when you miss home, I want you to remember something.

Home is never just a place.

Ohana ❤️
“Ohana means family, and family means no one gets left behind.”

And you know what?

You are my home.

My Home ♡ My home won't ever be a place.
My home has the most beautiful face.

My home, you're my home.

You're the one who makes me feel like a child.
You're the one who always makes me smile.

My home, you're my home.

So whenever you miss home, don't think about the distance.

Think about all the little things that make this home ours.

The stupid fights. The random conversations. The movie nights. The shopping trips. The songs. The laughter. The silly mischiefs. The ordinary days that somehow became our favourite memories.

All of those things are still here.

And so are we.

Whenever you miss home,
call me. ❤️

And I wouldn't mind sitting in silence with you, even for a whole day.

We don't even have to talk.

You can just call me, and I'll be there.

Because sometimes home isn't a house, a city, or a country.

Sometimes home is simply having someone who makes the distance feel a little smaller.

And no matter how far away you are, you will always have that here.

You will always have us.
You will always have me. ❤️

With all my love,
Sheryl ♡
