Open When...

You Need A Reminder That Everything Will Be Okay ❤️

Hellooo Akkaaa ❤️

First of all...

Take a deep breath.

And remember that life has its own way of surprising us.

Sometimes Life feels like this: “Oda oda oda dhooram kuraiyala Paada paada paada paattum mudiyala Poga poga poga onnum puriyala Aaga motham onnum velangala”

When things don't seem to go your way, even after all your hard work, remember that sometimes things happen for a reason we cannot see yet.

I know that's not always easy to accept.

So if it feels too hard to accept, think of it as an unexpected plot twist that god planned in your life's story.

The Plot Twist

At first, it might be annoying.

You might wonder, "Why did this have to happen?"

You might even wish you could rewrite the chapter.

But sometimes you have to keep reading before you understand why that chapter was there.

And maybe, when you finally reach the next few chapters, you'll look back and realise...

"Ohhh. So that's why." ❤️ The uncertainity in life is what makes it worth while.

So don't let one bad chapter convince you that the whole story is bad.

And About Progress...

If progress ever seems slow, remember:

Slow progress is still progress.

Just because you cannot see a huge change doesn't mean nothing is happening.

You are growing. You are learning. You are becoming better. Little by little.

So please remember to celebrate those tiny victories too.

You don't have to wait until you've reached the final destination to be proud of yourself.

Be proud of every little step that got you there.

And About Your Future...

Your future is in really good hands.

He is the best Author I have ever seen.

And I trust that He will make sure you become the person you are meant to be and fulfill the purpose He has for you.

You don't have to know the whole story. Just trust the Author.

You have already seen the miracles God has worked in your life.

So when things feel uncertain, remember all the times you thought something wouldn't work out... and somehow, it did.

And when what other people say hurts you, remember this:

They don't matter more than you do.

You are your own girl.

You don't have to live your life trying to make everyone else happy or prove yourself to everyone around you.

Let them talk.

You keep becoming the person you want to be.

🎵 And when you need a little reminder... “Sabki Rok Hai Tok Hai Nok Hai Jhok Hai Par Dil Mein Phir Bhi Hope Hai Hai Na, Hai Ha Life Thodi Hard Hai Andhe Ke Card Hai Hum Bhi Toh Star Hai Hai Na Ke Mana Iss Duniya Ki Hu Hi Nahi Main Apni Hi Duniya Bnaungi”

Keep the hope. Keep going. Keep being your own person.

Because you don't need to fit perfectly into somebody else's version of what your life should look like.

You can create your own world.

Your own story. Your own path. Your own little universe.

And if the world doesn't understand it, that's okay.

You don't need everyone to understand you.

You just need to keep going.

One day at a time.
One little victory at a time.
One chapter at a time. ❤️

And whenever you forget all of this, come back and read this letter again.

Remember that you have already survived so many things you once thought you couldn't.

You've made it this far.

And I know there is so much more waiting for you ahead.

So trust God.
Trust the process.
And most importantly, trust yourself.

And if you ever need someone to remind you of all of this...

You know where to find me. ❤️

No matter how far away I am, I'll always be cheering for you.

Through every plot twist, every tiny victory, every crazy chapter, and every beautiful surprise.

This isn't the end of your story, Akka.
It's just another chapter. ❤️

With all my love,
Sheryl ♡
