You Need To Know How Much You Mean To Me ❤️

Hellooo Akkaaa,

There is this song that always makes me think of us, because somehow its words feel so much like what I want to tell you. ❤️

“Catch my eye, take my hand This bond is tighter than we ever planned Give me courage, so I can land We know that divided we'll fall So together we stand”

I thank God every day for the gift of you.

You have made my life easier simply by being there. And the more I grow up, the more I realise just how blessed I am to have a sister like you.

Yes, we fight a lot. 😂 We have definitely had our moments.

But no matter what, you have always held my hand through it all.

Us ❤️
Always us ♡

Even though you were also figuring everything out for the first time, you somehow always made sure to make my way a little easier.

You are the person I look up to at all times. There are so many things about you that I admire, and I don't think I tell you that enough.

You mean so much to me.

Sometimes I feel bad thinking about how much you had to figure out on your own.

You were learning everything for the first time too, but somehow you still became the person who made things easier for everyone else.

I honestly don't know if I could have been as mature, strong, and amazing as you were.

And sometimes I think about how much easier things could have been for you if you had someone beside you to support you the way you have always supported me.

So I want to promise you something.

I will try my best to become someone you can rely on too.

Someone you can come to without having to pretend that everything is okay.

Someone with whom you can let your guard down.

Someone who will listen when you don't want to be the strong one anymore.

Because you have spent so much of your life giving to everyone around you.

And I hope that, little by little, I can become someone who makes sure you receive too.

You don't always have to be the one holding everyone's hand.

You can hold mine.

I'll be there.

Through the stupid fights, the serious conversations, the nonsense, the tears, the celebrations, and everything in between.

I don't know what life is going to look like years from now. We might live in different places, have completely different routines, and be busy with our own lives.

But I hope one thing never changes:

I'll always be your little sister,
and you'll always have me. ❤️

And whenever you forget how much you mean to me, come back to this letter.

You are not just my sister.

You are one of the biggest blessings God has given me.

And I will always be grateful that, out of all the people in the world, I got to call you my akka.

"The world's not perfect, but it's not that bad If we got each other, and that's all we have I will be your brother, and I'll hold your hand You should know I'll be there for you”

I Have everything I ever wanted Because I have you. ❤️

And I hope you always know that you have me too.

With all my love,
Sheryl ♡
